How to keep this going.

What your audience taught us

The first three seconds are everything. The reels that hooked people up front reached nearly twice as far and earned twice the saves. Open on a hook, not a warm-up.

Tutorials are your engine. Hair and skin how-tos are the content people save to come back and buy. Saves matter more than views.

Carousels are underused. Your feed posts save at twice the rate of your reels. Post more of them.

Shares come from heart. Your most-shared posts are the community and founder moments, not the tutorials. Keep both lanes running.

How to keep it sharp

Stay consistent. Tuesday and Thursday are your proven rhythm.

Keep it human. Raw, real, human-sounding content is what built this. Do not over-edit it into an ad.

Watch the right numbers. Track saves, shares, and link taps over views. Watch your three-second view rate as the early sign a hook is working.

Read monthly, adjust quarterly. Do not react to one post.
