08
Why organic social

Over 70% of the world is on social media for an average of 2.5 hours per day. We have never had this level of consumer attention in a single marketing channel. Our strategy captures that attention for Cincinnati Skin Center, and gets you in front of the audience that cares most about what you do. When you do that well, qualified leads follow. You cannot grow a business without people knowing about you. Organic social is where they are paying attention.

How the score works. Every metric is scored against Spark-stage ranges and marked exceeding, on track, or watch. You see the same set every month, so the comparison is always like for like.

A note on your follower count

A meaningful share of your current followers came in through giveaway promotions rather than through your content, so early engagement rates will read lower than the work deserves. We measure months one and two on audience fit and real conversations, not on rate against a number that was never yours.

The conversion lens

Right audience. Clear path. The right metrics. Get Cincinnati Skin Center in front of the right audience. Walk them through a clear path to booking. Track the metrics aligned to that path. With those three working, high-quality lead generation follows. Hard last-mile attribution is not running yet. We track signal first, attribution-grade second.
